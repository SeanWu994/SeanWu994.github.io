Shopify E-commerce Business

Project Type: Entrepreneurship / Personal Business

Tools: Shopify, TikTok, Instagram, Product Research

Project Overview

I started a small e-commerce business focused on pet products. My goal was to learn how to build an online store, identify customer needs, and promote products through different marketing channels.

I was responsible for selecting products, building the Shopify website, creating marketing content, and communicating with potential customers.

Business Problem

Starting a small online store comes with several challenges. I needed to identify products that customers might want, build trust with potential buyers, and find effective ways to reach customers without a large marketing budget.

What I Did

1. Product Research and Selection

I researched pet products and ordered samples to evaluate product quality before deciding what to offer in my store.

2. Shopify Website Development

I built an online store using Shopify, organized product listings, and worked on presenting the products clearly to customers.

3. Social Media Marketing

I experimented with TikTok and Instagram to promote products and share pet-related content.

I observed that TikTok generated more views and public engagement, while Instagram provided opportunities for direct conversations with potential customers.

4. Customer Feedback

I paid attention to customer comments, messages, and product interests to understand what worked and what needed improvement.

Results

  • Launched a functioning Shopify e-commerce store.
  • Evaluated product samples before listing products.
  • Tested two different social media marketing channels.
  • Received customer inquiries and initial orders.
  • Gained practical experience in online retail and customer communication.

What I Learned

This experience taught me that starting a business requires more than having a good idea. Product quality, customer trust, marketing, and execution all matter.

I also learned the importance of testing different approaches and using real customer feedback instead of relying only on assumptions.

Although this was a small business, it helped me develop an entrepreneurial mindset and a stronger understanding of how business decisions affect customers.
